# Afterlight progression

The quest book and JEI show the active objectives and recipes.

- **Ex Deorum sieving** provides basic resources, seeds, and materials for the first workshops.
- **Immersive Engineering Steel + PneumaticCraft Plastic** provide **Mekanism Steel Casings**.
- **Industrial Foregoing Fluid Extractor** provides Latex. Process it into rubber and **IF Plastic** using the current recipes shown in JEI.
- **IF Plastic** opens the Dissolution Chamber and **Simple Machine Frames**.
- **Ars Nouveau Imbuement Chamber** provides Source Gems. Crop growth supplies Source through an Agronomic Sourcelink; Source Gems become **Earth Essence**.
- **AE2 Logic Processor + Ars Earth Essence + Pink Slime + Simple Frame** provide **IF Advanced Machine Frames**.
- **Simple Frame + Steel Casing + Empowered Restonia + Deorum Ingot + Basic Table + crafting components** provide the **Extended Crafting Advanced Table**.
- **AE2 Engineering Processors** provide crafted **End Portal Frames**, opening the End and Dragon Hearts.
- **Ender IO End Steel** opens the **Elite Crafting Table** and contributes to the **Draconic Fusion Crafting Core**.
- **Draconic fusion** provides Awakened Draconium and the **Draconic Core** (`draconicevolution:awakened_core`).
- **Awakened Draconium + Mekanism Atomic Alloy + Elite Table + crafting components** provide the **Ultimate Crafting Table**.
- **Ars Ritual Brazier + Summon Wilden tablets** provide ordinary Wilden and their Horn, Spike, and Wing. Augment another ritual with one of each to summon the **Wilden Chimera**, which drops the **Wilden Tribute**.
- **Ultimate Table + Wilden Tribute + the materials below** provide the late **Philosopher’s Stone** through a tier-four 9×9 recipe.
- **ProjectE Transmutation Table, Condensers MK1/MK2, and Transmutation Tablet** lead to the cosmetic **Afterlight Beacon**.

## Skyblock access and player choice

All four Archwood Saplings have recipes from Oak Saplings and basic resources. The alternative Summon Wilden tablet uses a Vexing Archwood Log, Emerald Block, Iron Sword, Bow, and Lapis Block; it needs no Wilden drops. Ordinary summons supply the three Chimera ingredients. Build a separate arena because the boss damages blocks. Ars Caelum provides optional resource-island and geode rituals.

Create and Aeronautics, Oritech, Psi, Occultism and Ars Ocultas, space travel, equipment specialization, and Hephaestus Forge/Eternal Stella remain optional. Deorum still contributes to the Advanced Table. Resource crops, bees, and lasers are supply choices. Malum, Blood Magic, Neo Vitae, Nature’s Aura, Theurgy, and fluid cows are excluded. There is no ATM Star or expert mode.

Quests detect items without consuming them. Recipes enforce material gates. Rewards never supply the gated manufacturing products.

## Main quest chapters

1. **Starting Out:** Ex Deorum trees, composting, silk, sieves, water, lava, and a Cobblestone Generator.
2. **First Machines:** Immersive Engineering Steel and PneumaticCraft Plastic converge at the Mekanism Steel Casing; Industrial Foregoing Latex and Plastic lead to the Simple Machine Frame.
3. **Working Together:** AE2 Logic Processors, Ars Earth Essence, and IF Pink Slime produce Advanced Machine Frames. Actually Additions Empowered Restonia and Forbidden and Arcanus Deorum contribute to the Extended Crafting Advanced Table.
4. **Advanced Crafting:** AE2 Engineering Processors open End Portal Frames. Ender IO End Steel and Draconic Awakened Draconium lead toward the Extended Crafting Ultimate Table, with Mekanism Atomic Alloy.
5. **Afterlight:** Ars Wilden Tribute and Abjuration Essence join Draconic and industrial materials in the late Philosopher's Stone recipe. ProjectE then leads to the Afterlight Beacon.

The same quest appears through a link in its mod workshop. Tasks and rewards are
owned once, and the numbered chapters show the crafting path without forcing
optional power systems, Create, Oritech, or space travel.
