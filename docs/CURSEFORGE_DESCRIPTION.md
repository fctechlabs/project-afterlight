# Project Afterlight

Start with a tree, a small island, and a sieve. Build a world of industry and magic above the void.

Project Afterlight is a Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge skyblock pack inspired by the connected material progression of Project Ozone 3's Normal mode. A large quest book guides the essentials and individual mods while leaving room to choose your own machines, power, equipment, and building style.

## A connected campaign

Sieving supplies basic resources. Immersive Engineering Steel and PneumaticCraft Plastic connect early industry to Mekanism. Industrial Foregoing provides Rubber, Plastic, and machine frames. AE2 processors, Ars Nouveau essences, and Forbidden and Arcanus Deorum connect advanced manufacturing. End Steel and Draconic crafting lead into late Extended Crafting and ProjectE.

ProjectE's major unlocks require tier-four 9x9 Extended Crafting. Selected manufacturing milestones stay outside EMC transmutation. The final cosmetic Afterlight Beacon celebrates completing the campaign. There is no ATM Star or Kappa-style mode.

## Your island, your approach

- Repeatedly crouch near starter saplings and crops to grow them faster with Squat Grow.
- Choose **Default**, a small grassy island, or **Skyfactory**, a Dirt block and Oak tree.
- Use `/skyblock gui` from the signed lobby to create an island or manage your island team.
- Share progress through FTB Teams and protect builds with FTB Chunks.
- Explore magic through Ars Nouveau and its additions, Forbidden and Arcanus, Iron's Spells, and other optional branches.
- Expand resources with Mystical Agriculture, Productive Bees, Botany Pots, and automation.
- Build with Chisel, Chipped, Rechiseled, Macaw's mods, and a broad range of decorative and utility content.
- Access absent Overworld structure loot through crafted encounters, native boss drops, and targeted skyblock recipes.

**Create, Create Aeronautics, Oritech, Psi, Occultism, and space are optional.** The main campaign does not require them. Fluid cows, Nature's Aura, Theurgy, Malum, and Blood Magic are excluded.

## Current public release: 0.1.1

The first public release includes 3,612 quests across 72 chapters, two selectable island styles, custom material recipes, targeted skyblock access, and multiplayer support. Quests also have a 25% chance to award a bonus Chance Cube alongside their normal rewards. Balance, performance, and multiplayer testing are ongoing; expect fixes and changes. Back up worlds and keep every player on the same pack version.

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.251 · Java 21.** Start with 8 GB of allocated memory if your computer has enough available RAM. See the included installation, resource-access, progression, and performance guides.
