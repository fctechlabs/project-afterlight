# Project Afterlight

**Start with a tree. Build something worth getting lost in.**

Project Afterlight is a Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge skyblock pack about turning a tiny island into a home, a workshop, and eventually a world of industry and magic. Start with the basics, sieve your first resources, and grow into automated factories, spellcraft, and ambitious builds above the void.

Inspired by the connected material progression of Project Ozone 3's Normal mode, Afterlight gives you a campaign to work toward and plenty of room to make the island your own. A large quest book helps with both the essentials and individual mods, whether you enjoy following a goal or taking a detour to try something new.

## What you can build toward

- **From a sieve to a factory.** Turn early resources into steel, machines, power systems, and automated production.
- **Technology and magic that connect.** Use AE2, Ars Nouveau, Forbidden and Arcanus, and more as parts of a shared progression.
- **A quest book with room to explore.** Work through 3,612 quests across 72 chapters covering the campaign and individual mods.
- **More ways to grow your resources.** Build around Mystical Agriculture, Productive Bees, Botany Pots, and automation.
- **An island that feels like yours.** Decorate with Chisel, Chipped, Rechiseled, Macaw's mods, and a broad range of building tools.
- **A late-game goal.** Work toward Extended Crafting, ProjectE, and the final cosmetic Afterlight Beacon.

## A connected campaign

Sieving supplies basic resources. Immersive Engineering Steel and PneumaticCraft Plastic connect early industry to Mekanism. Industrial Foregoing provides Rubber, Plastic, and machine frames. AE2 processors, Ars Nouveau essences, and Forbidden and Arcanus Deorum connect advanced manufacturing. End Steel and Draconic crafting lead into late Extended Crafting and ProjectE.

ProjectE arrives late, with its major unlocks requiring tier-four 9x9 Extended Crafting. Selected manufacturing milestones stay outside EMC transmutation, so your production lines still have a job to do. The final cosmetic Afterlight Beacon celebrates completing the campaign.

## Your island, your approach

- Hold Shift and right-click Dirt or Grass with an empty main hand to gather pebbles, then craft four matching pebbles into their stone block.
- Repeatedly crouch near starter saplings and ordinary crops to grow them faster with Squat Grow. Mystical Agriculture crops and AE2 crystal growth are excluded.
- Choose **Default**, a small grassy island, or **Skyfactory**, a Dirt block and Oak tree.
- Use `/skyblock gui` from the signed lobby to create an island or manage your island team.
- Share progress through FTB Teams and protect builds with FTB Chunks.
- Explore magic through Ars Nouveau and its additions, Forbidden and Arcanus, Iron's Spells, and other optional branches.
- Expand resources with Mystical Agriculture, Productive Bees, Botany Pots, and automation.
- Build with Chisel, Chipped, Rechiseled, Macaw's mods, and a broad range of decorative and utility content.
- Access absent Overworld structure loot through crafted encounters, native boss drops, and targeted skyblock recipes.

**Create, Create Aeronautics, Oritech, Psi, Occultism, and space are optional.** Try them when they interest you; the main campaign does not require them.

## Getting started

1. Install the pack through CurseForge. Use Java 21 and start with about 8 GB of allocated memory if your computer has enough available RAM.
2. Create a **Skyblock Builder** world, read the lobby signs, and open **`/skyblock gui`**.
3. Pick **Default** for a small grassy island or **Skyfactory** for one Dirt block and an Oak tree.
4. Open the quest book and start building.

Playing with friends? Join the same Skyblock Builder island team to share a base, and the same FTB Teams party to share quest progress. FTB Chunks handles claims. Keep everyone on the same pack version.

## Feedback and issues

Found a broken recipe, a quest that won't complete, or a crash? [Send us a bug report](https://github.com/fctechlabs/project-afterlight/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include your pack version, whether you're playing alone or on a server, what happened, and how we can reproduce it. Screenshots and relevant logs help; check logs for personal information before posting.

Have an idea, a confusing moment, or feedback on the pacing? [Share a suggestion](https://github.com/fctechlabs/project-afterlight/issues/new?template=suggestion.yml). Tell us what you were trying to do and what would make it better. You don't need to arrive with a fully worked-out solution.

Please [check existing reports](https://github.com/fctechlabs/project-afterlight/issues) first and add details to a matching report when you can. Both forms use GitHub and require an account.
